# Shamiri Digital Hub

The operational back-end for the Shamiri Institute: programs, clinical screening, fellow
supervision and administration across hubs and schools in Kenya.

## Audience

Hub coordinators, supervisors, fellows, clinical leads and teams, and Shamiri administrators.
Each role has its own dashboard. They use it daily for scheduling, attendance, case management
and reporting, on laptops and on phones over mobile data.

## Goals

- Record and find operational data quickly: few clicks, dense tables, clear status.
- Stay fast on mid-range devices and slow networks: small pages, no decorative weight.
- Look like Shamiri: the One Shamiri brand, documented in DESIGN.md.

## Platform

web

## Constraints

- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text, a visible focus outline on every control.
- Clinical and student data is sensitive; screens show only what a role may see.
